Day by day

The driver’s earnings sheet, Oct 7–19, with the rides behind each day

The sheet is the driver’s own ledger, kept daily: what each app paid, tips, the time and miles each app counted as booked, the odometer, and what charging the car cost. The cards below show it day by day, plus what the ride-level record adds: when the day started and ended, how many rides, the biggest fare, how far it ranged. Booked time and miles are the apps’ own daily figures (accept to drop-off, so they exceed time with a passenger aboard). Unbooked miles are the odometer minus booked miles, so they include the commute from Covina and back as well as the gaps between rides; the map’s “empty miles” count only the gaps. Per-hour and per-mile figures here use the booked basis, which is why they sit below the per-trip-hour rates on the Earnings page.
